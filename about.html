About Dr. Arun Kumar

Dr. Arun Kumar is a trusted and reputed sexologist in India with over a decade of clinical experience in sexual medicine and men's health.

He has served as a senior consultant at a leading tertiary-care hospital and is recognized among respected specialists in sexual medicine. With experience caring for more than 10,000 patients, his work focuses on advanced diagnosis, personalized treatment and compassionate counselling for individuals and couples.

Academic Background

Dr. Arun completed MBBS followed by MD in Medicine. He further enhanced his expertise with DSM training in Sexual Medicine, giving him a strong academic foundation for erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation, infertility, hormonal imbalance and other sexual health concerns.

This medical background supports a world-class, diagnosis-led approach focused on effective, personalized and ethical care.

Why Choose Dr. Arun?

Patients choose Dr. Arun for ethical, confidential and result-oriented care across Agra, Delhi, North India and online consultations. His approach combines medical expertise with an understanding of psychological, relationship and lifestyle factors that influence sexual wellness.

The clinic focuses on long-term holistic solutions rather than shame-based advice or unrealistic quick-fix promises.

Memberships & Fellowships

Dr. Arun is associated with national and international learning networks and professional communities focused on sexual medicine, urology, endocrinology, assisted reproduction, hormone research and men's health.

These fellowships, certifications and professional affiliations reflect his commitment to staying updated with medical advances and offering evidence-based care for male infertility, erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation, low testosterone and complex hormonal imbalances.
