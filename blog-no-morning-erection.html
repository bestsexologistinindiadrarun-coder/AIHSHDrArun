Why Am I Not Getting Morning Erections Anymore?

Morning erections can change with sleep, hormones, stress and vascular health. Here is when it matters.

Primary keyword: no morning erection
Secondary keywords: morning wood absent, erection problems

What morning erections indicate

Morning erections are linked with normal sleep cycles, nerve function, blood flow and hormone rhythm. Their absence for a few days may not be serious, but persistent change deserves attention when it comes with weak erections or low desire.

Hormonal causes

Low testosterone, high prolactin, thyroid imbalance, obesity and poor sleep can affect morning erections. Testosterone is usually interpreted with symptoms and correctly timed blood testing.

Vascular and metabolic causes

Diabetes, high blood pressure, smoking and cholesterol problems can reduce penile blood flow. A change in morning erection pattern may be a useful reason to check overall metabolic health.

Sleep disorders and investigations

Poor sleep, sleep apnea, alcohol use, night-shift work and stress can suppress normal nocturnal erections. Evaluation may include glucose, lipids, testosterone, prolactin, thyroid profile and cardiovascular risk review.
