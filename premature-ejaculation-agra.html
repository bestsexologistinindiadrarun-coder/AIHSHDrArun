Understand the pattern

When is an assessment useful?

Occasional early ejaculation can happen. If it keeps happening and causes concern, a clinical discussion can help assess possible physical, psychological or relationship-related factors.

What to bring

  • Previous prescriptions or reports, if you have them.
  • A list of medicines, supplements or products already tried.
  • A note of when the concern began and whether it is consistent.
  • Your questions about treatment options and follow-up.

What the consultation can cover

Your history

Discuss onset, frequency, erection concerns, stress and previous treatment.

Individual assessment

The doctor decides whether examination or investigations are appropriate. Tests are not automatically required for everyone.

Options and review

Ask about possible medical or behavioural approaches, their limitations and when to review progress.
