Your consultation

Understand the pattern

Early ejaculation that keeps happening or causes distress is worth discussing with a doctor. Physical and psychological factors can contribute.

Tell the doctor when it began, how often it happens and whether there are erection concerns. Bring any previous prescriptions or products you have tried.

Patient information: NHS guidance on ejaculation problems

Common questions

For appointment times and fees, contact reception.

Is shighrapatan the same as premature ejaculation?

Shighrapatan (शीघ्रपतन) is commonly used for premature or early ejaculation. The consultation considers the pattern, control and impact on you.

Does everyone need the same treatment?

No. Your history and assessment guide the options. Do not start or combine medicines on the basis of a webpage.
