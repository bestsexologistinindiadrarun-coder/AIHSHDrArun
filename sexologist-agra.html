Start with the concern

What would you like to discuss?

  • Difficulty getting or maintaining an erection
  • Premature ejaculation / शीघ्रपतन
  • Low desire or hormonal health concerns
  • Male fertility, sperm count or motility concerns

Prepare for your visit

  • Bring previous reports and prescriptions, if available.
  • Bring a list of medicines and supplements you currently use.
  • Note when the concern began and what you would like to ask.
  • You do not need to order new tests before speaking with the doctor.

Before you book

Do I need to know my diagnosis?

No. Describe the concern in your own words. A webpage or self-assessment cannot establish a diagnosis.

Do I have to complete the online assessment?

No. You can call or request a consultation directly. The existing self-assessment is optional and is not a substitute for a doctor's assessment.

Does clicking WhatsApp book an appointment?

No. It opens a conversation with the clinic. Send your request and wait for the clinic to confirm your date and time.

For an appointment enquiry, share only the contact details and preferred time the clinic needs. Discuss medical details directly with the doctor. WhatsApp and Google Maps are external services with their own privacy terms.
