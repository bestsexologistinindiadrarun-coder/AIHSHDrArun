HCG and HMG Therapy for Male Fertility

When gonadotropin therapy may help male fertility and why monitoring is essential.

Primary keyword: hcg hmg therapy
Secondary keywords: gonadotropin treatment

Indications

hCG and hMG therapy may be considered in selected men with hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, pituitary-related hormone deficiency or fertility suppression patterns where stimulation of natural testicular function is appropriate.

Outcomes

The aim may be to support testosterone production and sperm formation. Response varies and often takes months because sperm production has a biological cycle.

Risks and monitoring

Treatment should be supervised with hormone tests, semen analysis, symptom review and safety monitoring. It is not a casual gym or self-medication protocol.

Fertility planning

Therapy decisions should consider couple goals, female partner factors, baseline semen report and whether assisted reproduction is also needed.
