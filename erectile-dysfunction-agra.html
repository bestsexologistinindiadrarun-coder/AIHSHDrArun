Your consultation

Look beyond one symptom

Repeated erection difficulties can have more than one contributor. Diabetes, blood pressure, some medicines, hormonal conditions, stress and anxiety may be relevant.

An individual assessment helps decide which checks and treatment options are appropriate.

Patient information: NHS guidance on erectile dysfunction

What happens next

A consultation, step by step

  1. Review your history

    Discuss when the difficulty occurs, medicines and relevant health conditions.

  2. Consider relevant checks

    The doctor decides whether examination or investigations are needed.

  3. Discuss the next step

    Understand suitable options, possible risks and follow-up.

Before your visit

Common questions

For appointment times and fees, contact reception.

What should I bring?

Bring existing prescriptions and reports, plus a list of all medicines and supplements. Tell the doctor about heart and blood-pressure medicines and any treatment already tried.

What if previous treatment has not helped?

Bring the prescription and describe how it was used, what changed and any side effects. The doctor can review the situation before discussing another approach.
