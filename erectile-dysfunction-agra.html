Assessment before treatment

Look beyond a single symptom

Repeated erection difficulties may have physical, psychological or mixed contributors. Diabetes, blood pressure, some medicines, hormonal issues, stress and anxiety can be relevant.

An individual review helps decide which checks and treatment options are appropriate. It does not mean everyone needs hormone treatment or the same tests.

Bring what you already have

  • Previous prescriptions and any diabetes, hormone or other reports.
  • A list of all medicines and supplements, including heart or blood-pressure medicines.
  • Details of treatment already tried and any side effects.
  • Your questions and a brief timeline of the concern.

What the consultation can cover

Health and history

Discuss when the problem occurs, medical conditions, medicines and lifestyle.

Relevant checks

The doctor may suggest an examination or investigations based on the history and findings.

A considered plan

Discuss suitable options, possible risks and follow-up. No medicine is suitable for every patient.

Common questions

Is erectile dysfunction always a testosterone problem?

No. There can be several contributors. The history and assessment determine whether hormone testing is appropriate.

What if treatment has not helped before?

Bring the prescription and explain how it was used, what changed and any side effects. Do not increase doses or combine products on your own.

Can I request an appointment without sharing symptoms online?

Yes. Call or send a general appointment request. You can discuss medical details directly with the doctor.
