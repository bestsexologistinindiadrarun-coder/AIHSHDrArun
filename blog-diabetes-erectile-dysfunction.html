Can Diabetes Cause Erectile Dysfunction?

How diabetes affects erections and why diabetic ED needs a root-cause medical plan.

Primary keyword: diabetes erectile dysfunction
Secondary keywords: diabetic ED treatment

Mechanism

Diabetes can damage small blood vessels and nerves that are essential for erection. It may also reduce testosterone, worsen fatigue and increase cardiovascular risk.

Diagnosis

Evaluation commonly includes diabetes control, blood pressure, cholesterol, kidney health, medicines, testosterone and lifestyle factors. ED may be one of the first signs that diabetes is affecting blood vessels.

Treatment

Treatment may include better metabolic control, weight management, exercise, medicine review, approved ED medicines when safe, hormone correction when indicated and counselling for confidence and relationship stress.

Why early care matters

Ignoring diabetic ED can delay detection of vascular risk. A medical plan protects both sexual health and overall long-term health.
